# Internal AI Processing and Independent Reuse of Trade Books: Contract triggers and author remedies in U.S. publishing

Anhui Zhang and Shuliang Hong, equal first authors. Version 0.12 (13 October 2026).

Author preprint and reproducibility companion. Not peer reviewed, not accepted by a journal, and not a version of record. The uploader account is not an additional author.

The main argument separates permission for a specified internal publishing task or upload from safeguard performance, provider assent and author enforcement. N1 is the principal, limited coordination illustration; N2/N3 are optional. The archive preserves uncertainty about assent, preemption and relief. It contains no observed executed AI agreements, measured market failure, simulated human validation or intercoder-reliability result.

## Contents

- manuscript/: the author preprint.
